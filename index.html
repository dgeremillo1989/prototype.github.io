WiiJump

An interactive, motion-controlled capstone system designed to track, analyze, and gamify vertical jump mechanics in real-time.

Project Overview

WiiJump bridges the gap between physical fitness and immersive gaming environments. Built as our senior capstone project, this platform leverages hardware motion sensors to register precise user jump heights, hang-time data, and landing impact forces.

Core Features

Precision Motion Tracking

Utilizes signal processing to filter noise and map physical jumps cleanly onto our virtual display interface.

Real-Time Analytics

Instantly calculates vertical leap distance, continuous velocity curves, and estimated caloric burn metrics.
